# Developer: onboarding

**Archetype:** [Developer](README.md) · Sketch

## Steps

1. Work through `docs/setup/` end to end: provision, Access, the email provider, sending-domain DNS, wiring the archive to a website, and verify.
2. Deploy, and confirm `/api/version` reports the expected build.
3. Hand off to a publisher, possibly themself, once the verify checklist passes.
